# Tutorial Center Africa | Class Operations & Tutor Assessments SOP

**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-002  
**Version:** 2.0 (Production Live)  
**Applies to:** Academic Operations, Course Advisors, Lead Tutors  
**Primary Channel:** WhatsApp Community & Tutorial Center Web App (`tutorialcenter.africa`)  
**Operating Window:** Evening Sessions starting promptly at 5:00 PM (Consecutive blocks: 5:00–6:00 PM, 6:00–7:00 PM, 7:00–8:00 PM)

---

## 1. The Golden Rule of Class Confirmation

By **30 minutes before every scheduled class start time**, the tutor, students, and management must know definitively whether the class will hold:

* **For the 5:00 PM Class:** Decision is finalized and reported **by 4:30 PM**.
* **For the 6:00 PM Class:** Decision is finalized and reported **by 5:30 PM**.
* **For the 7:00 PM Class:** Decision is finalized and reported **by 6:30 PM**.

### Key Ground Rules
1. **The 1-Student Threshold:** If even **one (1) student** confirms on the day of the class, the class will hold (even if they specify an expected late arrival time).
2. **Strict Cutoff (40 Minutes Prior):** Student confirmation closes **exactly 40 minutes before class start** (4:20 PM, 5:20 PM, 6:20 PM).
3. **No Reopening:** An unconfirmed student, "unsure" reply, or no reply does not count as confirmation. If nobody confirms by the 40-minute cutoff, the class is cancelled. **A late reply received after cancellation does not reopen the class.**

## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Previous Night (By 8:00 PM) — Evening Forecast Poll
* The **Follow-Up Advisor** posts tomorrow’s scheduled subjects and start times in the WhatsApp Community.
* Students vote on all subjects they expect to attend.
* **Mandatory Notice Included in Post:** State clearly that this evening vote is an estimate, and a new live confirmation poll will be posted on WhatsApp the next day to lock in attendance.

### Step 2: By 3:30 PM — First Class (5:00 PM) Confirmation Poll
* The **Follow-Up Advisor** posts the confirmation poll to the WhatsApp Community group and DMs students on WhatsApp (including those who voted the night before).
* Ask students to select their exact expected arrival time using real clock times.

### Step 3: By 4:20 PM — Call Unconfirmed Students (The 4:20 Cutoff)
* The two advisors split the roster so every unconfirmed student receives one phone call attempt.
* Start early enough to finish all calls by **4:20 PM**.
* Record the exact answer given by phone. If unanswered, rely on any poll answer already submitted earlier in the day.

### Step 4: Before 4:30 PM — Complete Record & Report to Management
* The **Follow-Up Advisor** compiles the confirmed student names and their expected arrival times.
* Send the formal Class Decision Report to the COO in the Advisor WhatsApp group, tagging the CEO and the other advisor. If nobody confirmed, state that clearly.

### Step 5: By 4:30 PM — Attendees Alerts & Tutor Notification
* The **Follow-Up Advisor** notifies both the students and the tutor whether the class will hold:
  * **If Holding:** Confirm the start time and notify the tutor of any expected late arrivals.
  * **If Cancelled:** Inform the tutor immediately and demand an acknowledgement reply on WhatsApp. If the tutor does not reply within 5 minutes, call them directly to confirm receipt.

### Step 6: By 4:40 PM — Open Classroom in the App
* For a confirmed class, the **Lead Ops Advisor** opens the scheduled lesson on the Tutorial Center website (`tutorialcenter.africa`) and verifies that students can enter.

### Step 7: By 4:50 PM — Tutor Readiness & Audio Check
* The assigned tutor joins the classroom in the app.
* The **Lead Ops Advisor** verifies the tutor's microphone, camera, and whiteboard/screen-sharing setup. If the tutor is absent by 4:50 PM, the Lead Ops Advisor must inform the COO immediately.

### Step 8: At 5:00 PM — Live Class Launch
* The **Lead Ops Advisor** directs students into the room and monitors attendance using the portal schedule page, comparing live attendees against the confirmed roster.
* If nobody joins at the expected arrival time, contact the confirmed students and inform the COO.

## 4. Running Multi-Class Evenings (6:00 PM, 7:00 PM, etc.)

When classes run consecutively (e.g., 5:00–6:00 PM, 6:00–7:00 PM, 7:00–8:00 PM), advisors run the staggered relay:

1. **At 5:00 PM (Start of 5:00 PM Class):**
   * **Lead Ops Advisor:** Remains inside the 5:00 PM live room managing the session.
   * **Follow-Up Advisor:** Immediately posts the confirmation poll for the **6:00 PM class** on WhatsApp.
2. **At 5:20 PM (The 20-Minute Escalation Mark):**
   * The **Follow-Up Advisor** calls unresponsive students scheduled for the 6:00 PM class.
   * **CRITICAL PROTOCOL FOR STUDENTS CURRENTLY IN THE 5:00 PM CLASS:**
     * **Do NOT call their phone directly**, as it will disrupt their ongoing live lesson audio.
     * Send them a direct message on WhatsApp first.
     * If they do not respond on WhatsApp, notify the **Lead Ops Advisor** inside the live 5:00 PM classroom to ask the student directly (via chat or mic): *"Joy, will you be staying for the 6:00 PM class?"*
3. **Before 5:30 PM (Decision Deadline):**
   * The **Follow-Up Advisor** compiles the 6:00 PM report, submits it to the COO in the Advisor WhatsApp group, and sends the **Attendees Alert** to the 6:00 PM students and tutor.
4. **By 5:40 PM:**
   * The **Follow-Up Advisor** opens the 6:00 PM classroom in the web portal and verifies student access.
5. **By 5:50 PM:**
   * The 6:00 PM tutor joins the room for audio and video verification.
6. **At Exactly 6:00 PM (The Turn of the Hour):**
   * The 5:00 PM class ends; the 6:00 PM class goes live.
   * **Advisor Role Swap:** The advisor who prepped the 6:00 PM room takes over as **Lead Ops Advisor** for the live 6:00 PM session. The other advisor becomes the **Follow-Up Advisor** and immediately drops the confirmation poll for the **7:00 PM class** on WhatsApp.
   * Repeat the exact sequence for 7:00 PM: 6:00 PM poll $\to$ 6:20 PM calls $\to$ 6:30 PM decision & report $\to$ 6:40 PM room open $\to$ 6:50 PM tutor sound check $\to$ 7:00 PM class launch.

## 6. Staff Coverage, Duties & Strike Policy

1. **Role Division:** The two advisors have equal standing. Before 3:30 PM each day, advisors agree who owns **Lead Ops (in-portal room management)** and who owns **Follow-Up (WhatsApp polls, calls, reports)**.
2. **Duty Coverage Rules:**
   * If you need your colleague to take over a duty, notify them with the deadline. You own the task until they explicitly reply and accept.
   * If they have not replied **20 minutes before a deadline** (e.g., before 3:10 PM for the 3:30 PM poll), notify the COO immediately.
   * If they have not accepted **10 minutes before the deadline**, perform the task yourself and report it to management.
3. **Strikes & Accountability:**
   * A strike is an official warning for missing a duty in this SOP (e.g. late poll posting, unmade phone calls, missing the 30-minute COO report, or failing to check the tutor by the 10-minute mark).
   * If a class duty is missed and neither advisor communicated, **both advisors receive a strike**.
   * If one advisor became unresponsive, the other must report it and cover the task to avoid receiving a mutual strike.
   * **Three-Strike Rule:** On the **third strike in a calendar month**, management enforces a formal administrative penalty. Strike counts reset on the 1st of each calendar month.

## 7. Daily Register & Quality Review

* **Daily Log:** Keep one private record per class. Before class, enter the date, subject, start time, advisors, tutor, students’ replies, expected arrival times, decision, and notification times. After class, record actual opening time, tutor arrival, student attendance, and any missed duty.
* **Weekly Review:** Every five teaching days, the COO reviews which classes opened on time, who confirmed and attended, which classes were cancelled, and how often school or travel prevented students from confirming on time.
